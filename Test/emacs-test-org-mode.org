* Important Notes
** Note 1
~wolframscript -rawterm~ does not allow **empty lines**, which will be automatically **removed** before running codes!

Use "\n" to insert empty lines in a string.

#+name: sci-wolfram-empty-lines-in-string
#+begin_src wolfram :exports both
str1 = "To be, or not to be, that is the question:

Whether 'tis nobler in the mind to suffer

The slings and arrows of outrageous fortune,

Or to take arms against a sea of troubles,

And by opposing end them?"

str2 = "To be, or not to be, that is the question:\n
Whether 'tis nobler in the mind to suffer\n
The slings and arrows of outrageous fortune,\n
Or to take arms against a sea of troubles,\n
And by opposing end them?"
#+end_src

** Note 2
For async session block in org-mode version < 9.8,
*spaces* before a string (i.e. str="    Hello") will be automatically *removed*
due to the default ~filter-prompts~ option in ~org-babel-comint-async-register~ function.

Since org-mode version 9.8, a new option [[https://orgmode.org/Changes.html][disable-prompt-filtering]] was introduced to avoid this situation.

#+begin_src wolfram :async no :exports both
Print["         spaces before a string"]
#+end_src

#+RESULTS:
:results:
         spaces before a string
:end:

#+begin_src wolfram :async yes :exports both
Print["         spaces before a string"]
#+end_src

#+RESULTS:
:results:
spaces before a string
:end:
